# Discord & Voice Chat Setup (Call Mode Beta)

How to let Capri join your voice channels and speak directly to your friends in **Discord, Steam, Teams, or Zoom**.

## 1. Virtual Audio Routing (So Friends Hear Capri)
To route Capri's voice into your voice chat without speakers bleeding into your mic:
1. Install **VB-CABLE Virtual Audio Device** (free driver) or use **Steam Streaming Microphone**.
2. Open Capri **Settings → VOICE → Her voice in your mic** and select `CABLE Input (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)`.
3. Open **Discord → User Settings → Voice & Video** and set **Input Device** to `CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)`.

## 2. Recommended Discord Audio Settings
Disable Discord's built-in filters so they don't clip Capri's speech:
- **Echo Cancellation:** `OFF`
- **Noise Suppression (Krisp):** `OFF`
- **Automatic Gain Control:** `OFF`

## 3. Voice Commands in Call Mode
- *"Hey Capri, join the Discord call and tell them I'll be back in 10 minutes."*
- *"Capri, launch whatever game they are playing."*
- Press **Esc** at any time to immediately stop an active desktop action.
