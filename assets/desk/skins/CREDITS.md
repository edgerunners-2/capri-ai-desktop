# Skin art

- `capri/`: her official look (standing, sitting, eyes closed), drawn for Capri by **xilia_world00** on Discord.
- `watcher/`: the Watcher (a secret skin), drawn for Capri by **xilia_world00** on Discord.

A skin is a folder with the same sprite sheets as `capri/` (same names, sizes and frame layout). Add it to
`capri/skins.py` to list it in her Skins menu.
Lying-down poses (lay_*.png) by xilia_world00 (Discord).
